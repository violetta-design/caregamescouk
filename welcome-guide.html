Welcome to Together

a small guide to getting started

Together is a set of gentle games to help you spend meaningful time with someone living with dementia. There's nothing to install from an app store, no account to make, no fees. Just open the link and you're in.

"There are no wrong answers. Just stories."

Step one — open the link

You should have been sent a web link. Tap it on your phone or tablet, or paste it into your browser. The link will look something like this:

[ your Together link will go here ]

It opens straight away. The first time, it'll ask for the name of the person you're spending time with, and their pronouns — that's just so the games feel personal. You only do that once.

Step two — add it to your home screen

This is the trick that makes it feel like a real app. Once it's on the home screen, you tap a Together icon — the same way you'd open WhatsApp or anything else.

📱 On an iPhone or iPad

1. Open the link in Safari (the blue compass icon).

2. Tap the share button ⬆︎ at the bottom of the screen.

3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

4. Tap Add. You'll see a Together icon appear on your home screen — that's it.

📱 On an Android phone or tablet

1. Open the link in Chrome.

2. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right.

3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

4. Confirm. The icon appears on your home screen.

Step three — choose a game

From the home screen, you'll see seven games. They all work the same way: you read a card aloud, ask the prompts, and listen. Tap how it went — Great, Good, or Skip — and the next card appears. Each session can be as long or short as feels right.

  • I've Been There — places spark stories.
  • Memory Game — gentle recall, one round at a time.
  • Songs of a Life — music opens doors that words can't.
  • The Five Senses — a smell, a sound, a taste.
  • Faces of the Family — add real photos. The most personal one.
  • The Way We Did It — let them teach you how it was done.
  • Then & Now — a lifetime of changes, side by side.

A few gentle tips

There's no scoring or right answer. The "Great / Good / Skip" buttons are just for you, to help you notice what they're enjoying. Not for them.

Sit beside, not opposite. Hold the phone at a comfortable angle. This isn't a quiz — it's a conversation. Their face matters more than the screen.

Skip freely. If a card doesn't land, move on. Some days a particular memory won't be there, and that's fine.

The Faces game is the most powerful — and the most work. Set aside 15 minutes one quiet evening to add 8-10 photos with names, relationships, and a one-line note for each. After that, it's the game most people return to.

Photos stay private. Anything you add is stored only on the device you added it on. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

If something goes wrong

If the link doesn't open, or the home screen icon disappears, just open the link again — your settings and any photos you added will still be there on the same device. The app remembers everything for you.
