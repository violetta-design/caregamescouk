About the Creator

Built from the inside out

"I built these apps because I couldn't find anything gentle enough — tools that felt human, not clinical."

I'm a live-in carer. Every day I sit with the person I care for, and I know firsthand how hard it can be to find the right thing to do in a quiet moment — something that feels meaningful, not just time-filling.

Care Games Co began as a personal project to make those moments easier. Together started as a small collection of games I made for the person I care for. WithYou grew out of all the notes, guides, and little routines I'd built up over months of caring.

Both apps are designed to be gentle, intuitive, and genuinely useful — for carers working in agencies, for families at home, and for anyone who wants to feel a little less alone in the caring role.
