What to do in difficult moments

Try the first topic free. Tap it to open. Ideas and support for carers, not medical advice.

🍽️
Won't Eat
Food
1
Don't make it a confrontation — stay calm and warm.
2
Offer a favourite food or something very small — even a biscuit counts.
3
Check if they're not hungry, or if something else is wrong (pain, sadness, constipation).
4
Try a different time — hunger often comes and goes.
5
If refusing for 2+ days, let the GP or care agency know.
Remember: Forcing food never works and causes distress. A small amount eaten happily is better than a large meal refused.
